# Lara AI

> AI-powered employee experience platform that helps HR teams automate tasks, measure engagement, and strengthen the relationship with every employee.

Lara AI is the AI agent for People teams. It accompanies every employee throughout their journey, automates key HR processes, and delivers actionable people analytics.

## Modules

### Employee Experience
Measure engagement and employee experience in real time with continuous listening, custom surveys, and actionable People Analytics.
- URL: https://meetlara.ai/employee-experience.html

### Helpdesk
AI-powered help desk that automatically answers employee FAQs and manages requests. Over 80% of queries automated.
- URL: https://meetlara.ai/helpdesk.html

### Onboarding
End-to-end automated onboarding flows for new hires: communications, reminders, surveys, and task tracking.
- URL: https://meetlara.ai/onboarding.html

### Comunicaciones
Personalized employee communications via WhatsApp, Slack, Teams, and more. Schedule messages, celebrate birthdays, and segment audiences.
- URL: https://meetlara.ai/comunicaciones.html

### People Analytics
Turn people data into robust, actionable reports. Custom dashboards for every role, industry, and organization type.
- URL: https://meetlara.ai/people-analytics.html
